In the Japanese drama Welcome Back, First Love (おかえり、初恋。), two classmates who were always on their own in high school meet again at a reunion ten years later. So much of what happens now answers a day from back then. Flip the cards below to see the same moment, ten years apart.

Episode 1A blond delinquent, reunited after 10 years

THEN · HIGH SCHOOL

Ice pop

In the middle of class, Taguchi suddenly says he wants an ice pop. When Shiori says the school shop won’t be open now, he thinks for a second:

じゃあ抜け出すかThen let’s sneak out.

NOW · TEN YEARS LATER

Ice pop

Ten years later, on a hot day, her office hands out ice pops. The moment she takes one, she thinks of him again.

ふとした瞬間に思い出すIt comes back to me in unguarded moments.

THEN · HIGH SCHOOL

Side by side

Unable to fit in with her class, Shiori escapes to an empty spot and finds blond Taguchi already claiming it. When she says she can’t fit in with everyone, he says:

じゃあ俺と一緒だなThen you’re just like me.

NOW · TEN YEARS LATER

Side by side

At the reunion ten years later, she’s sitting alone in a corner again. He walks over and asks:

座っていい？Mind if I sit here?

THEN · HIGH SCHOOL

Ear piercing

The first time they talk, she tells him his earrings look nice. He asks if she wants to touch them; flustered, she says she couldn’t.

似合ってて素敵だと思うThey suit you. I think they look lovely.

Ear piercing

He’s dyed his hair black and taken out the earrings, but the piercing holes are still there. This time, she really does reach out and touch.

おかえり、田口君Welcome back, Taguchi.

THEN · HIGH SCHOOL

Once more

After sneaking out of class together for ice pops, just before they part laughing, he says casually:

じゃあまた抜け出すかLet’s sneak out again sometime.

NOW · TEN YEARS LATER

Once more

Ten years later, it’s her turn to work up the courage and ask if they can be close again. He leans in, and his answer is a little more than she expected:

Lunch

At lunch in high school, he looks at her tiny bento, asks if it’s enough, and hands her a bread roll from the convenience store:

なあ、弁当そんなに足りんの？Hey, is that little bento really enough?

Lunch

Ten years later, at lunch together, he asks the same thing, then feeds her a bite from his own plate:

相変わらずそれで足りんの？Still eating just that much?

The book

Telling him about a novel she loves, she accidentally spoils it and hurries to apologize. He says:

そうやって説明してくれた方が読んだ時が楽It’s easier to read once you’ve told me about it like that.

The book

Ten years later, he pulls out the newest volume of the same series:

山根さんがきっかけで読むようになったんだよYou’re the reason I started reading, Yamane.

THEN · HIGH SCHOOL

The character like him

She says her favorite character in the novel is a bit like Taguchi. As she starts to explain what she likes about him, he cuts in, embarrassed:

NOW · TEN YEARS LATER

The character like him

Ten years later, he’s the one who asks. She blushes and can’t get the words out:

どこが好きなんだっけ？So what was it you liked about him?

THEN · HIGH SCHOOL

See you tomorrow

As lunch break ends, he says he’ll probably be here after school too, and leaves her with:

NOW · TEN YEARS LATER

See you tomorrow

One night ten years later, she waits for him at the office and tells him what she’s kept inside for so long:

THEN · HIGH SCHOOL

Apart

Back in high school, he once told her coldly:

NOW · TEN YEARS LATER

Apart

Ten years later, he confesses, but she can’t bring herself to answer. At last she says what she’s afraid of:

もう離れるのは、嫌だI don’t want us to be apart again.

THEN · HIGH SCHOOL

One bite

Sharing food in high school, he says the point isn’t just having someone there:

誰かじゃなくて誰と食べるかよIt’s not about eating with someone. It’s about who you eat with.

NOW · TEN YEARS LATER

One bite

On a date ten years later, she gives him a bite, and he remembers this happened before:

山根と食べるとおいしいよFood tastes good when I eat with you, Yamane.

THEN · HIGH SCHOOL

Holding hands

At a summer festival in high school, they held hands once. That night, when they reach the fork in the road, she waves and says:

じゃあ私あっちだから、また学校でねI go this way. See you at school.

NOW · TEN YEARS LATER

Holding hands

Ten years later, he admits his heart was racing when they held hands at the festival. On their date, when someone tries to chat them up, this time she’s the one who takes his hand:

こうすれば、もう、声かけられないよねThis way, nobody will try to talk to us again, right?

THEN · HIGH SCHOOL

Like

On the festival night in high school, walking beside him, this is what she thought to herself:

それでも、隣にいられればよかったEven so, just being by your side was enough.

NOW · TEN YEARS LATER

Like

Ten years later, she gives the feeling a name and says it out loud:

田口君のことが、好きですI like you, Taguchi.

The original manga

The drama is based on the manga of the same name by Kamemizu Tora (かめみずとら). It first appeared on X in 2021; as of October 2026 the Japanese edition is up to volume 7, and it’s still ongoing. A Traditional Chinese edition is published in Taiwan by Ching Win Publishing (青文出版社).

With only 30 minutes a week, the manga is a nice way to fill the wait between episodes.

FAQ

How many episodes does Welcome Back, First Love have, and when is the finale?

It has 8 episodes. It airs on MBS in Japan every Thursday at 1:29 a.m. late night (technically early Friday, Japan time). If there are no breaks, the finale, Episode 8, airs late Thursday, November 26.

What do the “ten years ago” and “ten years later” parts of Welcome Back, First Love show?

Shiori Yamane and Ryosuke Taguchi were the two kids in their high school class who were always on their own. Ten years later, they meet again at a class reunion. The story moves back and forth between high school and now, and many present-day scenes echo something that happened back then.

What is Welcome Back, First Love based on?

It is based on the manga of the same name by Kamemizu Tora (おかえり、初恋。). It first appeared on X in 2021; as of October 2026 the Japanese edition is up to volume 7 and still ongoing. A Traditional Chinese edition is published in Taiwan by Ching Win Publishing.
