      FAQ

      What time zone are the release times in?

      Times follow the broadcaster: China time (UTC+8) for Chinese dramas and Japan time (UTC+9) for Japanese dramas. Netflix in your country may release on a different day.

      What do the gray dates mean?

      The official schedule has not reached those days yet, so we estimate them from the current pace. We update the dates when the platform announces breaks or extra episodes. If you hear first, use "Adjust the estimate" to change episodes per update, break days and paid early access.

      Why are VIP members ahead?

      Chinese streaming platforms let higher membership tiers watch a few episodes early. Switch the platform buttons to see where each one is.

      Will my calendar update if the schedule changes?

      No. Come back to this page and download it again. No sign-up needed.
